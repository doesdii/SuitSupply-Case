Flags and exposure

Flag keys come from the tracking plan. The prototype has panel switches that stand in for them.

Stop ruleThe Custom Made PM sets trousers-design-options to OFF within one working day when Speed, Keep, or Operations fails. OFF gives every customer today's flow. The per-option rule switches off one option only.

Fails open: if the flag service fails, the customer gets today's flow (US-11, gap E5). A customer lookup that fails or takes more than 300 milliseconds shows the baseline, with no late card (US-18). Neither is in the prototype: its address default is f=on.

Adoption, Keep, safety, and money read the order system, so customers who decline analytics still count. Returns, cancellations, and expectation answers join back to the test through flag_variant on the order line. A next order links through the order system's own customer key for Come back, the later repeat-order metric. The key never goes into an event.

Returns are allowed under today's 30-day policy. A changed pair cannot go back into stock, so every returned changed pair is a write-off. Split returns by reason, so a size return does not count against the detail.
